# KILNSONG — Throw clay, glaze it bright, fire ten commissions

One wheel, one kiln, one verb at a time. Throw clay on the potter's wheel, glaze it bright, and fire ten commissions — and break THE CRACK, the kiln's three-phase flaw, in the final firing.

A complete original browser game for make.fun best-game-01: ten commissions from Little Bowl to THE GREAT URN, three glaze colours, a kiln that spikes when you least want it, three hearts, and thirty stars to earn.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No network requests, no external files.

- Mouse: drag the clay to shape it (sideways widens, up pulls taller). Click a glaze, then click a band to paint it. Hold the mouse to vent the kiln.
- SPACE: vent the kiln (hold). ENTER: start / next commission / submit a phase. R: restart. P: pause. M: mute.
- WIN: fire all 10 commissions and break THE CRACK. LOSE: run out of hearts.
